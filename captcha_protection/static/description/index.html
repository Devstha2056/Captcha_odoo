Captcha Protection

Protect your login, signup, password reset, Contact Us and Helpdesk ticket forms with Google reCAPTCHA (v2 or v3) — stop spam and bot submissions with one module and a single reCAPTCHA credential.

Login & Signup

Verifies reCAPTCHA on /web/login, /web/signup and /web/reset_password before the request is processed.

Contact Us

Adds reCAPTCHA to the standard Website Contact Us form to block spam messages.

Helpdesk Tickets

Protects the Helpdesk website ticket submission form from automated abuse.

⚙️ Configuration

Enter your Google reCAPTCHA site key and secret key (v2 or v3) once in the Captcha Protection settings — verification is then applied automatically across all supported forms.

Requires Odoo Enterprise Edition (Website Helpdesk).
